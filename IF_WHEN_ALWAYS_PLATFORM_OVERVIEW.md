# If When Always Platform - Comprehensive Overview
*Enterprise-Grade Collaborative AI Intelligence Platform*

---

## **Executive Summary**

The If When Always Platform (formerly SymbiosoAi ThinkTank) is an enterprise-grade collaborative intelligence platform that revolutionizes decision-making through structured multi-agent AI debates and comprehensive brainstorming capabilities. The platform orchestrates specialized AI agents to conduct rigorous analytical debates, generate actionable solutions, and produce professional reports across three progressive complexity levels designed to meet diverse organizational needs.

---

## **Platform Architecture & Technology Stack**

### **Core Infrastructure**
- **Frontend**: React with TypeScript, shadcn/ui components, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Express.js with TypeScript in ESM configuration  
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM (Neon serverless)
- **AI Integration**: OpenAI GPT-4 with Anthropic Claude backup
- **Authentication**: Replit OpenID Connect with session management
- **Real-time**: WebSocket integration for collaboration
- **Mobile-First Design**: Responsive across all device types

### **Multi-Agent AI System**
The platform employs specialized AI agents, each with distinct roles and perspectives:

**Core Debate Agents:**
- **Analyst**: Data-driven analytical perspectives with evidence-based reasoning
- **Critic**: Challenges assumptions and presents alternative viewpoints  
- **Synthesizer**: Builds consensus and integrates different perspectives
- **Domain Expert**: Contributes specialized knowledge across 18+ domains

**Brainstorming Agents:**
- **Solution Architect**: Designs practical, implementable solutions
- **Implementation Specialist**: Focuses on execution and resource planning
- **Innovation Catalyst**: Generates creative alternatives and approaches
- **Integration Specialist**: Synthesizes perspectives into unified strategies

**Report Generation Agents:**
- **Executive Summarizer**: Creates high-level strategic synthesis
- **Technical Writer**: Produces comprehensive documentation
- **Research Analyst**: Manages citations and expert analysis
